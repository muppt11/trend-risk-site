Every step of the calculation

The “safe” production window is feasible only under your supplied scenario.

  1. 01Lead time = 21 manufacturing + 7 sourcing + 14 transit + 7 buffer = 49 calendar days.
  2. 02Arrival = 2026-10-02 + 49 days = 2026-11-20. Arrival-day demand is included.
  3. 03Remaining forecast = 2754.10 units, distributing each uploaded period uniformly across its inclusive calendar days.
  4. 04Adjusted demand = 2754.10 × (1 − 20 / 100) = 2203.28 units. The haircut is your scenario assumption.
  5. 05Supported new units = max(0, floor(2203.28 − 0 inventory available at arrival)) = 2203.
  6. 06Latest viable arrival = 2026-11-23; latest order = arrival − 49 days = 2026-10-05.
  7. 07Scenario inventory exposure = max(0, 2000 − 2203) × $12.50 = $0.00. This is inventory cost exposure, not a measured loss or avoided cost.

Manufacturing, sourcing, transit and buffer durations are added sequentially. If activities overlap, enter non-overlapping durations. No holidays, business-day calendars, discounts, salvage values, stockout penalties or probability estimates are inferred.
